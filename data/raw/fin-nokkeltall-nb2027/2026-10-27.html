    Key figures in the National Budget for 2027

    Press release | Date: 07/10/2026 | Ministry of Finance

    • Les på norsk
    • | Loga sámegillii

    To ensure equal access to macroeconomic figures that might be market sensitive, the Ministry of Finance publishes selected key figures prior to presenting the National Budget at 10am. More detailed forecasts will be published in the Budget.

    The Norwegian economy is thriving; however there has been a slight decline in growth the past year. The Norwegian economy is affected by the current global unrest and expectations have risen in relation to price growth and interest rates in the past six months. At the same time, wages have increased more than prices, and this trend is expected to continue in both 2026 and 2027. The improvement in purchasing power lays the foundation to expect increased growth in private consumption and overall economic activity moving forward.

    Through the fiscal policy framework for 2027, the Government will continue to govern with sound financial management. The Government proposes to spend BNOK 608.4 of the Government Pension Fund, which includes BNOK 85 in support to Ukraine. Fund spending increases with BNOK 4.9 from this year measured in fixed 2027 prices, and corresponds to 2.7 % of the value of the Government Pension Fund Global. This is in line with the fiscal policy guideline and well below the expected real return of 3%. Measured as a share of the trend GDP for mainland Norway, fund spending amounts to 12.6 %. This is unchanged from this year, and the budget indicator is 0.0. The budget proposal is estimated to have a neutral effect on the activity in the economy next year.
